0001About

I'm a Project Research Associate at RISE Lab, IIT Madras, on Shakti — India's open-source RISC-V processor initiative. I work in Bluespec SystemVerilog mainly on SoC peripherals and bus fabrics — AXI4 and APB interconnect — with some contributions to the processor core, and I test the designs and write their C drivers.

Alongside, I'm pursuing an MS by Research in Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Madras, focused on computer architecture and systems for deep learning: building RISC-V SoCs with Chipyard and Shakti, designing convolution and systolic-array accelerators, bringing them up on Xilinx FPGAs, and compiling real models to them with TVM and ONNX.

Before IIT Madras I studied Electronics and Communication Engineering at Sri Venkateswara College of Engineering and worked as a software engineer at Eurofins IT Solutions on .NET, Angular and Dynamics AX. That mix — digital logic at one end, production software at the other — is what draws me to the hardware–software boundary.

2026 · CS6651 course project

CNN Accelerator on a Shakti RISC-V SoC — Drowsiness Detection

Parameterised Bluespec convolution engine (configurable MAC lanes, int8 data with int32 accumulation) integrated into the Shakti SoC and run on a Nexys Video FPGA. Models are compiled from ONNX with TVM to C, and accelerator and CPU-only builds are validated against each other on hardware via OpenOCD/GDB.
